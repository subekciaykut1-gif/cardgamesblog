## The First Move Advantage

In What to Do When You're Stuck in Solitaire: Basics & Rules, your first few moves set the tempo for the whole session. Always focus on uncovering face-down cards in the deepest piles first to open up your moves. This uncovering phase is where the most critical mistakes are made by casual players.

## Managing the Foundation

Avoid the temptation to move every card to the foundation too fast. Sometimes, keeping an Ace or 2 in the tableau allows you to maneuver other cards. This is a subtle nuance of What to Do When You're Stuck in Solitaire: Basics & Rules. If you move a card up and find you required it to move a King later, you've trapped yourself completely.
